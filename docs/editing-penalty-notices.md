# Editing penalty notices

The assigned case owner can edit each notice under **Penalty notices — wording review**, after saving the proposed decision and before submitting for approval.

1. Edit the subject and body for the offending club, reporting club or officials. Add, remove or change wording as needed; keep the section headings and substantive content. Check the text against the recorded sanctions, dates and appeal instructions above.
2. Select **Save notice wording** before editing another notice. Saving records a new revision and audit event. Validation errors retain the attempted wording for correction or copying.
3. Use **Preview saved PDF** to review the saved version. The reporting-club version must remain free of confidential case material and reporter identity.
4. Review all three notices and select **Submit reviewed notices for approval**. Edits lock at submission. Approval preserves the saved text in the emails and PDFs for Denver's final sign-off.

A stale page cannot overwrite a newer revision or submit wording that has changed since it was reviewed. Revising the underlying decision creates fresh notices tied to that decision; previous revisions remain in history.

Wording about a manual message from Denver is editable, but editing that sentence does not record delivery, suppress automatic emails or change penalties. Issued correspondence remains immutable.
